1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how No WiFi Brain Puzzles handles user information. No WiFi Brain Puzzles is an offline mini puzzle game designed for casual gameplay.

2. Information We Collect

No WiFi Brain Puzzles does not require users to create an account and does not ask for personal information such as name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera access, microphone access, or payment information.

The app may store basic gameplay data locally on your device, such as coins, level progress, high scores, settings, sound preferences, and daily challenge progress. This information is used only to provide gameplay features and improve the user experience inside the app.

3. Local Game Data

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. This data is not used to personally identify you. If you uninstall the app or clear app data, this local progress may be deleted.

4. Advertising

No WiFi Brain Puzzles may display ads using third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob. Advertising services may collect and process certain device and usage data to provide, limit, measure, and improve ads.

These third-party services may use identifiers, cookies, or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws. Users in regions where consent is required may be shown a consent message before personalized ads are used.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies. These services may include:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services

Please review the privacy policies of these third-party services for more information about how they handle data.

6. Internet Access

The core gameplay of No WiFi Brain Puzzles is designed to work offline. Internet access may be used for optional features such as showing ads or accessing external links such as this Privacy Policy.

7. Children’s Privacy

No WiFi Brain Puzzles is intended for a general audience. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information directly with third parties, except where necessary for third-party services such as advertising, app functionality, legal compliance, or security.

9. Data Security

10. User Choices

You can control certain app settings such as sound, vibration, and gameplay preferences from within the app. You can also reset or delete local app data by clearing the app data from your device settings or uninstalling the app.
