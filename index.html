Privacy Policy — No WiFi Brain Puzzles

Last updated: October 3, 2026

DreamLexicon provides No WiFi Brain Puzzles (com.nowifi.brainpuzzles). Contact: DreamLexicon Support.

Offline gameplay and local records

No WiFi Brain Puzzles does not require an account. It stores game progress, partial boards and sessions, settings, coins/hints, scores, daily challenge state and mechanism/maze selections locally to resume play and display progress. The 3D maze and mechanism controls run on the device. The app does not operate a developer game-progress server.

The app does not ask for your name, email, contacts, camera, microphone, payment details or GPS location for gameplay. A puzzle code identifies a game/level, not a player identity. When you choose Copy puzzle code, Android's clipboard receives that code; you decide where to paste it.

Advertising and privacy choices

This app uses Google AdMob. Its advertising services can process IP-derived approximate location, advertising/device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse. Our app does not send your saved record contents as ad parameters. Google handles this information under its own policies.

The app uses Google's consent system. Eligible advertising requests follow its consent state. Where required and available, Settings offers an advertising privacy form; Android also provides advertising privacy controls. Choosing a different advertising mode does not necessarily stop all technical data processing. The app's offline features remain available when advertising is unavailable.

Google privacy information: Google Privacy Policy.

Internet access and retention

The puzzle games work offline. Internet access can be used by advertising and when you open an external link such as this policy or the store page. An external website handles its visitors under its own policy.

Use the app's reset controls to reset the selected game data. Some preferences can remain. Android Settings > Apps > No WiFi Brain Puzzles > Storage > Clear storage removes local app data. Uninstalling may remove local progress. Android backup or device transfer may include app data depending on the device's settings. Backup copies and data held independently by Google advertising services are controlled separately.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience puzzle collection. It does not directly request personal information for a gameplay account. Third-party advertising can still process technical information as described above. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, contact DreamLexicon Support.

Support, rights and security

If you contact DreamLexicon, we receive the message, your contact details and any information you choose to send. Use dreamlexicon.sup@gmail.com for access, correction or deletion requests about information held by DreamLexicon. Do not send passwords or unnecessary private records. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Data held independently by third-party services is governed by their own policies. Those services may process information outside your country. Depending on your location, additional privacy rights and a right to complain to your data-protection authority may apply. No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure.
